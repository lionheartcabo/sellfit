Stripe restricted businesses

Stripe’s prohibited and restricted businesses page is the reject list payment processors enforce after you have already built. It names industries Stripe will not serve, and categories that need extra due diligence. Ordinary software and SaaS are not on that list. sellfit checks a one-line idea against the cited rules and prints the quote.

Official source (canonical): https://stripe.com/legal/restricted-businesses · Last updated — May 13, 2026. Quotes below are copied from data/policies/2026-08.json, fetched 16 August 2026. They were not invented. (25 Stripe rules in the dataset: 1 allowed, 15 prohibited, 9 restricted_review.)

What Stripe’s list covers

The page lists businesses that cannot use Stripe, plus restricted categories that need more review. Unlisted ordinary software is the cash-register case sellfit treats as allowed.

stripe-scope allowed unlisted-ordinary-business

Stripe's list covers specific prohibited and restricted categories; ordinary software/SaaS is not listed.

“This page provides information on the types of businesses, products, and industries that can’t use Stripe’s services.”
