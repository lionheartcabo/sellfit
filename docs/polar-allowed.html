What Polar allows

Polar’s Acceptable Use Policy names digital product classes that are generally acceptable. sellfit marks matching ideas allowed when the worst product-fit hit is one of these — not a promise Polar will approve your account or pay out.

Official source (canonical): https://polar.sh/legal/acceptable-use-policy · Effective Date — March 25, 2026. Quotes below are copied from data/policies/2026-08.json, fetched 16 August 2026. They were not invented. eBooks sit on the digital-products list and the restricted list — see Polar restricted review.

All 4 allowed rules in v0

These are the acceptable-product lines Polar publishes. Restricted and prohibited lists still apply on top. Instant digital fulfillment is part of what Polar expects to sell as Merchant of Record.

polar-a-software allowed software-saas

Polar lists Software and SaaS as acceptable products you may sell.

“Software & SaaS;”

https://polar.sh/legal/acceptable-use-policy · effective 2026-03-25 · checked 2026-08-16

polar-a-digital allowed digital-products

Polar accepts digital products such as templates, eBooks, PDFs, and code.

“Digital products: Templates, eBooks, PDFs, code, icons, fonts, design assets, photos, videos, audio, and similar digital assets;”

https://polar.sh/legal/acceptable-use-policy · effective 2026-03-25 · checked 2026-08-16

polar-a-premium allowed premium-content

Polar accepts premium content and access such as courses and GitHub repo access.

“Premium content & access: Discord server access, GitHub repository access, courses, and content requiring a subscription.”

https://polar.sh/legal/acceptable-use-policy · effective 2026-03-25 · checked 2026-08-16

polar-a-fulfillment allowed digital-fulfillment

Acceptable Polar sales are digital goods or services Polar or your API can fulfill immediately.

“Generally, acceptable services are digital goods, software, or services that can be fulfilled by (1) Polar on your behalf (License Keys, File Downloads, GitHub or Discord invites, or private links) or (2) your site/service using our APIs to grant immediate access to digital assets or services for customers with a one-time purchase or subscription.”

https://polar.sh/legal/acceptable-use-policy · effective 2026-03-25 · checked 2026-08-16

Check your idea

Offline. No network. From the sellfit directory (Python 3.10+). Humans get the text table. Agents add --json for one JSON object and nothing else.

Human text

python -m sellfit check "MIT-licensed CLI that checks Polar AUP"
python -m sellfit cite polar-a-software

For agents

python -m sellfit check "MIT-licensed CLI that checks Polar AUP" --json
python -m sellfit cite polar-a-software --json
