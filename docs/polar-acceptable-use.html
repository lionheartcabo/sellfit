Will Polar accept my product?

Polar is a cash register for software. It takes the card, remits tax, and pays you for digital goods it can fulfill. It is not a bank for human services and not a store for physical boxes. sellfit checks a one-line idea against the Polar Acceptable Use Policy effective 25 March 2026 and prints the citing quote.

Official source (canonical): https://polar.sh/legal/acceptable-use-policy · Effective Date — March 25, 2026. Quotes below are copied from data/policies/2026-08.json, fetched 16 August 2026. They were not invented.

What Polar is for

The AUP lists software you may sell. Polar (or your API) has to be able to fulfill the sale immediately — license keys, file downloads, invites, or private links. That is the cash-register test.

polar-a-software allowed software-saas

Polar lists Software and SaaS as acceptable products you may sell.

Two frequent rejects

Item 8 is the outreach ban. AI generators are not banned outright; they sit on the restricted-review list and may still be refused.
