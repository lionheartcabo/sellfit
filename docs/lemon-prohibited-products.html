Lemon Squeezy prohibited products

Lemon Squeezy is a cash register for digital goods — software, SaaS, files it can fulfill on its own site. The prohibited-products page is the reject list people hit after they have already built. sellfit checks a one-line idea against that page and prints the citing quote.

Official source (canonical): https://docs.lemonsqueezy.com/help/getting-started/prohibited-products. Quotes below are copied from data/policies/2026-08.json, fetched 16 August 2026. They were not invented. The official page printed no effective date; the dataset uses last_checked.

Guides: sellfit dataset · Polar acceptable use · Lemon Squeezy prohibited products · Stripe restricted businesses · Chrome Web Store program policies

What Lemon allows

Digital goods fulfilled through Lemon’s website, including software and SaaS. That is the cash-register test, same shape as Polar.

lemon-accept-digital allowed digital-goods

Lemon Squeezy generally allows digital goods fulfilled through its website.

“As a general rule, we allow selling of digital goods that can be fulfilled through Lemon Squeezy’s website.”

https://docs.lemonsqueezy.com/help/getting-started/prohibited-products · checked 2026-08-16

lemon-accept-software allowed software-saas

Software and SaaS are listed as acceptable Lemon Squeezy products.

“Software & SaaS”

https://docs.lemonsqueezy.com/help/getting-started/prohibited-products · checked 2026-08-16

Human services of any kind, including marketing and consulting, are prohibited.
