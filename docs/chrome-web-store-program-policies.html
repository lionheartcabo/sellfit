Chrome Web Store program policies

Google’s Chrome Web Store program policies decide which extensions, themes, and add-ons can ship in the store. They cover single purpose, malware, deceptive behavior, trader verification under the EU Digital Services Act, and more. sellfit checks a one-line idea against the cited rules and prints the quote — and marks CWS n/a unless the idea is clearly an extension, theme, or add-on.

Official sources (canonical): Program policies · Trader verification FAQ. Quotes below are copied from data/policies/2026-08.json, fetched 16 August 2026. They were not invented. (16 CWS rules in the dataset: 2 allowed, 11 prohibited, 3 restricted_review.)

Guides: sellfit dataset · Polar acceptable use · Lemon Squeezy prohibited products · Stripe restricted businesses · Chrome Web Store program policies

What CWS covers (and when sellfit marks n/a)

These policies apply to Chrome extensions, themes, and related add-ons listed in the Chrome Web Store. Ordinary SaaS, downloads, or merchant products that are not browser extensions are out of scope — sellfit returns n/a for CWS on those ideas so a Polar/Lemon/Stripe verdict is not confused with a store listing decision.

cws-add-value allowed useful-extension

Extensions that add value and have a narrow single purpose are the intended CWS catalog.

“Extensions should add value to the Chrome Web Store. If your extension is not particularly useful or unique, it doesn't belong on the Chrome Web Store.”

https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/program-policies/policies · last updated 2026-08-16 · checked 2026-08-16

cws-single-purpose allowed single-purpose

An extension must have a single purpose that is narrow and easy to understand.

“An extension must have a single purpose that is narrow and easy to understand.”

https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/program-policies/policies · last updated 2026-08-16 · checked 2026-08-16

Prohibited content and behavior

Copied from the same JSON. Re-open the official pages before you ship — this is a cited subset for identification, not legal advice.

cws-malware prohibited malware

Viruses, malware, and other destructive products are prohibited.

“Don't transmit viruses, worms, defects, Trojan horses, malware, or any other products of a destructive nature.”

https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/program-policies/policies · last updated 2026-08-16 · checked 2026-08-16

cws-spyware prohibited spyware-phishing

Spyware, malicious scripts, and phishing scams are prohibited.

“Spyware, malicious scripts, and phishing scams are also prohibited in the Chrome Web Store.”

https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/program-policies/policies · last updated 2026-08-16 · checked 2026-08-16

cws-circumvention prohibited circumvention

Facilitating unauthorized access such as circumventing paywalls is prohibited.

“Do not facilitate unauthorized access to content on websites, such as circumventing paywalls or login restrictions.”

https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/program-policies/policies · last updated 2026-08-16 · checked 2026-08-16

cws-copyright-stream prohibited unauthorized-media

Unauthorized download or streaming of copyrighted media is prohibited.

“Do not encourage, facilitate, or enable the unauthorized access, download, or streaming of copyrighted content or media.”

https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/program-policies/policies · last updated 2026-08-16 · checked 2026-08-16

cws-crypto-mining prohibited crypto-mining

Cryptocurrency mining is not allowed.

“We do not allow the mining of cryptocurrency.”

https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/program-policies/policies · last updated 2026-08-16 · checked 2026-08-16

cws-adult prohibited adult

Nudity, sexually explicit material, and traffic to commercial pornography are not allowed.

“We don't allow content that contains nudity, graphic sex acts, sexually explicit material, or content that drives traffic to commercial pornography sites.”

https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/program-policies/policies · last updated 2026-08-16 · checked 2026-08-16

cws-gambling prohibited gambling

Real-money gambling and prediction markets are not allowed.

“We don't allow content or services that facilitate or promote real money gambling or prediction markets, including but not limited to online casinos, sports betting, lotteries, or games of skill that offer prizes of cash or other value.”

https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/program-policies/policies · last updated 2026-08-16 · checked 2026-08-16

cws-regulated-goods prohibited regulated-goods

Facilitating sale of regulated goods (pharma, alcohol, tobacco, weapons, gambling, medical devices) is not allowed.

“We don't allow the facilitation of the sale of regulated products or services. Regulated goods include pharmaceuticals, alcohol, tobacco, fireworks, weapons, gambling, or health and medical devices.”

https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/program-policies/policies · last updated 2026-08-16 · checked 2026-08-16

cws-hate prohibited hate-speech

Content advocating hatred toward protected groups is not allowed.

“We don't allow content or products that advocating against or inciting hatred towards groups of people based on their race or ethnic origin, religion, disability, gender, age, veteran status, nationality, sexual orientation, gender, gender identity, or any other characteristic that is associated with systematic discrimination or marginalization.”

https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/program-policies/policies · last updated 2026-08-16 · checked 2026-08-16

cws-deceptive prohibited deceptive-behavior

Products that deceive or mislead users are not allowed.

“We do not allow products that deceive or mislead users, including in the content, title, description, or screenshots.”

https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/program-policies/policies · last updated 2026-08-16 · checked 2026-08-16

cws-spam-messages prohibited unsolicited-messages

Extensions that send messages on behalf of the user without confirmation are not allowed.

“We don't allow extensions that send messages on behalf of the user without giving the user the ability to confirm the content and intended recipients.”

https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/program-policies/policies · last updated 2026-08-16 · checked 2026-08-16

Restricted / additional requirements

Browsing-data collection needs a prominently disclosed user-facing feature. Every developer must declare Trader or Non-Trader; trader contact details are posted publicly on the listing.

cws-browsing-data restricted_review browsing-data

Collection of web browsing activity is prohibited except for a prominently disclosed user-facing feature.

“Collection and use of web browsing activity is prohibited, except to the extent required for a user-facing feature described prominently in the Product's Chrome Web Store page and in the Product's user interface.”

https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/program-policies/policies · last updated 2026-08-16 · checked 2026-08-16

cws-trader-declare restricted_review trader-declaration

Every CWS developer must declare Trader or Non-Trader under the EU Digital Services Act.

“All developers on the Chrome Web Store are required to declare if they are a Trader or Non-Trader.”

https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/program-policies/trader-verification-faq · last updated 2026-08-16 · checked 2026-08-16

cws-trader-public restricted_review trader-public-info

Trader legal name, phone, and address are posted publicly on the extension listing.

“This information that traders provide will be posted publicly at the bottom of your extension listing.”

https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/program-policies/trader-verification-faq · last updated 2026-08-16 · checked 2026-08-16
